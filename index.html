Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

TCG Game Planner is built with an offline-first, private architecture. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal data, accounts, telemetry, or behavioral profiles. All your decks, match history, and card collection records are stored strictly in an encrypted/private SQLite database on your device.

2. Scryfall Public API

The app queries Scryfall (api.scryfall.com) exclusively to retrieve public card text, printings, imagery, and market price statistics. No user identification, token, or personal details are transmitted.

3. iCloud Sync & Backups

If you choose to export or back up your data to iCloud Drive, your files reside exclusively inside your private Apple iCloud container managed by your Apple ID.

4. On-Device Speech & Camera

Voice dictation notes are processed on-device using Apple Speech framework. Camera or photo library access is used solely to capture images of your physical cards for alterations.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, please reach out via GitHub Issues.
