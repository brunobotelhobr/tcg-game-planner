## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I back up my decks and collection?
Go to **Settings > Database Backup & Restore**:
- Tap **Export Backup** to save a standalone `.sqlite` file to your Files app, AirDrop, or local drive.
- You can restore this backup anytime on any iPhone, iPad, or Mac running TCG Game Planner.

### How does card pricing work?
Card market prices are fetched from Scryfall. In the Collection tab, tap the **Update Data** button in the toolbar to fetch the latest bulk price indices.

### Can I plan sideboarding for different formats?
Yes! TCG Game Planner comes with built-in metagame archetypes and support for Pre-Modern, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Commander/EDH, Pauper, Pioneer, and Standard. You can also define custom formats and archetypes directly from the Metagame tab.
