Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours

Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short

TCG Game Planner does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you choose to back it up or send it somewhere.

Who we are

TCG Game Planner ("the app") is made by Bruno Botelho, an independent developer. Questions about this policy: open a ticket from the Support page.

Data that stays on your device

The app keeps everything you enter in a database on your device: decks, your collection, sideboard plans, formats and archetypes, tournaments and their notes, the life counter, your profile (name, e-mail address and photo, if you add them) and photos you attach to cards. None of it is sent to us. We have no servers and no way to see it.

What the app connects to

  • Scryfall (scryfall.com), the source of card data, images, rulings and prices. Requests contain the card names and searches needed to answer them, and the technical information every internet request carries, such as your IP address and the app's name and version. Nothing identifies you to Scryfall beyond that. Scryfall's own privacy policy applies to its servers.
  • Card stores (LigaMagic, CardTrader, Card Kingdom, StarCityGames), only when you open a card's store page. Those pages are the stores' own websites, and their privacy policies apply.
  • GitHub, only if you follow a link to this site's repository or open a ticket. Tickets are public and governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Beta Features

Beta Features are off for everyone. They work only if you unlock them and add your own Google credentials. When you use them:

  • The AI assistant and AI card digitization send your messages, the files you attach, the photos you choose and the parts of your data the assistant needs to answer to Google Gemini, using your API key.
  • Financial Sync sends one number a day (your collection's value in US dollars), the date and an identifier you choose to the Google BigQuery project you set up, using your service-account key.

That data goes to your own Google account under Google's terms. Your keys stay on your device and are never included in backups.

Backups

Backups are files the app writes to a folder you choose, such as a folder in iCloud Drive. Files in iCloud Drive are stored by Apple under your Apple Account, according to Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to them. Backups you export go wherever you send them.

Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: to take or choose a photo of an altered or signed card, or your profile picture. Photos stay on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to dictate notes. Speech is turned into text on your device.

The app asks for each permission only when you first use the feature. You can refuse, or change your mind later in your device's Settings.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app deletes its data. On the Mac, also delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/TCGGamePlanner. Then delete any backup files from your backup folder.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and the release notes say what changed. If the app ever starts to collect data, this page and the App Store listing will say so before it happens.
