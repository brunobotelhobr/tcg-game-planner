Version 0.1

Coming soon

The first release, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  • Decks as a list or as card stacks, with mana costs, value and what you still need.
  • Sideboard plans for every archetype, on the play and on the draw, the sideboard matrix, and exports to a spreadsheet and a PDF guide.
  • Formats and archetypes by tier, with banlists from Scryfall.
  • A collection by edition, finish, condition and language, with decks reserving the copies they use; exports as a card list or CSV.
  • Tournaments round by round, analytics by deck and by opponent, and a shareable report as images or PDF.
  • A life counter for two to six players.
  • Prices, editions, rulings, banlists and images refreshed in one Update.
  • Daily backups to a folder you choose, such as iCloud Drive.
TCG Game Planner
