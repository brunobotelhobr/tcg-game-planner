Getting started

The app opens with sample data so every screen has something to show: a few formats and their archetypes, an example deck with sideboard plans, a tournament and a small collection. Change it, or delete what you do not need.

On iPhone the app has five tabs: Collection, Decks, Metagame, Tournaments and Analytics. Settings opens from your profile picture (or initials) at the top left of each tab. On iPad and Mac the same sections, and Settings, are in the sidebar.

The app follows your device's language. To choose one, go to Settings › Language: System, English or Português (Brasil).

Settings › About shows your app version and links back to this site: the guide, the FAQ, support and the policies.

Your profile

Settings › Player Profile holds your name, e-mail and photo. When you add a photo you can move and zoom it inside a round frame.

  • Your photo or initials open Settings from every tab on iPhone.
  • The life counter and its history show your name instead of "Me".
  • Tournament reports show your name, e-mail and photo.

The profile stays on your device and travels in your backups. It is never sent anywhere.

Collection

The Collection tab has four lists:

ListWhat it holds
CollectionThe copies you have in hand, and your proxies.
On the WayCopies you bought that have not arrived.
WantCopies you are looking for, plus the copies your decks still need.
ArchiveCopies you own but keep out of play. Decks never take archived copies.

Adding a card

  1. Tap + and type the card's name. Portuguese names work too; the card is saved under its official English name.
  2. Set how many you have, have on the way, want, play as proxies or keep in the archive.
  3. Choose the condition (M, NM, SP, MP, HP, D), language and finish, and mark it as signed or altered.
  4. Pick the edition from every printing Scryfall knows. "Owned Only" shows the editions you already have.

An altered or signed card can carry your own photo, cropped to the card's shape.

Stacks

Each combination of edition, finish, condition and language is its own row, or stack. Four Revised and four Fourth Edition copies of a card are two stacks: they are different things to own, at different prices.

Finding cards

Search by name, then use the filter button beside the search field to filter by deck, show only cards in decks, missing ones or cards without an edition, change the sort, or open the advanced filters (colors, mana value, type and subtype, set, artist, rules text, format, condition, language, foil, signed, altered, Reserved List and tags). The toolbar switches between a list and a grid of card art.

Value

The overview card totals the list in US dollars at each stack's own edition and finish. A price shown as ~$ is an estimate: there is no price for that exact finish, so the closest one is used. A card with no price counts as zero.

Exporting

The share button on each list's overview card offers:

  • Copy List and Share List — the rows on screen as text, one per line, such as 4x Lightning Bolt (LEA).
  • Export CSV — the same rows with every field the app stores, one column each: quantities, condition, language, edition, set, artist, prices, the card's text and stats, and its legality in every format.

Both follow your current search and filters.

Decks

Create a deck from the Decks tab and give it a name, format and featured card. Add cards one by one, or use Import List in the deck's menu to paste a list in the usual format, one card per line, such as 4 Lightning Bolt. Export List copies it back out the same way.

Cards are grouped into creatures, sorceries and instants, enchantments, artifacts, others, lands, sideboard and maybeboard. The maybeboard holds cards you are considering: it never counts toward the deck's size, price or completeness.

The view menu switches between a List, a List With Card Art and Card Stacks, where each section fans out as real cards.

How decks use your collection

A deck reserves copies from your collection: first the copies in hand, then proxies, then copies on the way. A copy reserved by one deck is not free for another, so the app can tell you exactly what you would have to move between decks.

  • Missing means no free copy covers that slot.
  • In Decks on the Collection tab counts the copies decks are holding.
  • Picking a specific stack for a card ties the slot to that stack, for example a particular foil.
  • Archiving a deck releases everything it reserved. Deleting it does too.

Snapshots

Snapshots in the deck's menu saves the current list so you can compare it later or go back to it.

Sideboard plans

Open a deck and choose Sideboard Plans. Pick an opponent archetype, then the cards that come in and go out on the play and on the draw, with a note on the matchup. The plan warns when the ins and outs do not balance.

The Sideboard Matrix shows every plan at once: a row per card, a column per archetype and play or draw. Tap a cell to change it. From there you can export the whole matrix as a spreadsheet (.xlsx), and from the plans list as a PDF sideboard guide.

Metagame

The Metagame tab holds your formats and their archetypes, ranked by tier, each with its colors and a featured card. Archetypes you play against become the columns of your sideboard matrix and the rows of your analytics.

Every format has an "everything else" archetype for the decks you do not track one by one. Banlists and card legality come from Scryfall and are refreshed with each Update.

Tournaments

Create an event with its name, date and the deck you played. Players and Final Placement are optional, but the report needs both to show your standing, such as "5th of 18".

Log each round: the opponent's deck, the opponent's name, games won and lost (or a bye) and your notes. You can dictate notes; speech is turned into text on your device.

Edit Tournament also holds three texts for the report: why you chose the deck, your mistakes and takeaways, and general notes.

Tournament report

The report turns the event into something to share: your profile, the deck, placement and record, every round, and optionally the texts, your decklist and the full sideboard guide. Choose the sections, then share it as phone-sized images (for chats and social media) or as a PDF.

Analytics

  • By Deck — one of your decks: its win rate and record, and the matchup breakdown against every archetype of its format, including the ones you have never faced. Tap a matchup to see those rounds.
  • By Opponent — everyone you have noted by name, with your win rate and record against each. Tap a name to see your latest rounds against them, and which of your decks you played. Only rounds with the opponent's name filled in count.

Both can be limited to a period (the last 30 or 90 days, this year or a custom range) and sorted by win rate or number of matches. Byes are left out of the matchup and opponent breakdowns.

Life counter

Open it from the Tournaments tab (the twenty-sided die). It handles two to six players, a starting life of 20, 30, 40 or any number, poison counters, a color per player and a table layout for face-to-face play.

The history groups quick taps on the same player into one change, so tapping minus four times reads as −4. Filter it to see only your changes or only your opponents'.

Card lookup

Look Up Card finds any card: its text, mana cost, editions and prices, and its official rulings. Store links, here and on every card's details, open the card on LigaMagic, CardTrader, Card Kingdom and StarCityGames inside the app.

Keeping card data current

The Update button (the circular arrow) refreshes, in one pass, the prices, editions, rulings and banlists of every card in your collection and decks, then downloads the card images you are still missing. It also runs on its own once a day when you open the app.

Update downloads Scryfall's daily card file, so the first run on a new device takes a few minutes and is best done on Wi-Fi. Everything else in the app works offline.

Backups and new devices

Settings › Backups saves a copy of your data once a day (and, with Backup on change, again after you edit). It keeps one backup per day for the Retention Period you choose.

On iPhone, choose an iCloud Drive folder.

Until you pick a folder under Destination, iPhone backups stay inside the app on that phone, and the app says "Local folder only". On the Mac the default is iCloud Drive › Sideboard Planner.

Pick the same iCloud Drive folder on each of your devices. With Prompt newer on launch on, the app offers to restore when it finds a newer backup made on another device. The app does not sync live: it restores a backup, which replaces the data on that device.

By hand

Export Backup saves a .sqlite file you can keep or send (AirDrop works). Import Backup restores one. Backups from older versions of the app open too.

What a backup contains

  • Included: decks, collection, tournaments, formats and archetypes, sideboard plans, snapshots, the life counter, your profile and the photos you attached to cards.
  • Not included: the downloaded card data and images (Update gets them again), app preferences such as the language, Beta Feature keys and files attached in the Assistant.

On the Mac

  • ⌘ N creates a deck; ⌘ 1 to ⌘ 6 switch sections (also in the Go menu).
  • Exports open the standard Save panel.
  • Your data lives in ~/Library/Application Support/TCGGamePlanner.

Beta Features

Beta Features are experimental tools that are off for everyone: an AI assistant, AI card digitization from a photo, and a daily sync of your collection's value to Google BigQuery. They need an unlock code (Settings › Beta Features) and your own Google keys, are not supported, and may change or go away.

When you use them, what you send goes to Google under your keys and Google's terms. See the Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

  • A card has no edition, mana cost or image. Run Update. Opening the card or its deck also fetches what is missing.
  • Update failed. Check your connection and try again later; Scryfall may be busy.
  • A backup does not show up on another device. Make sure both use the same iCloud Drive folder and that iCloud Drive has finished syncing the file.
  • Your app version is in Settings › About.

Still stuck? Open a ticket.
