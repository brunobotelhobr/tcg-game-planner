Features

Everything around the table, in one place

Build the deck, plan the sideboard, play the event, learn from the results. Your collection sits underneath, so you always know which copies you have free.

Decks

A list or real card stacks, with mana costs, the deck's value and which cards you still need.

Sideboard plans

What comes in and goes out against every archetype, on the play and on the draw. Export a spreadsheet or a PDF guide.

Metagame

Formats and their archetypes by tier, with banlists kept current. Edit them to match your local scene.

Collection

Every copy by edition, finish, condition and language. Decks reserve the copies they use, so two decks never count on the same card.

Tournaments

Log each round, the opponent and your notes. Share a report as phone-sized images or a PDF.

Analytics

Win rates by deck against every archetype, and your record against each opponent you have played.

Life counter

Two to six players, poison counters and a history that groups quick taps into one change.

Card data that stays current

Prices, editions, rulings and banlists from Scryfall, refreshed in one tap or once a day on its own.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. Backups go to a folder you choose. Nothing is collected.

On the Mac and iPad

Room for the whole picture

On larger screens the sections sit in a sidebar, decks fan out as card stacks and the sideboard matrix shows every matchup at once.
